# BRIEF: Judah Oka portfolio, filing cabinet

Interviewed (Judah, 2026-10-13). Answers from his message, two reference images, and four multiple-choice decisions.

## The eight topics

1. **Vibe and references.** A 21st.dev "stacking glass cards" GSAP component: cards stack as you scroll, and earlier ones shrink back with their edges peeking above. Also his notebook sketch: a "Work History" folder with stacked files and a "Details" card pulled out of it, annotated "scroll to shuffle through" and "scrolling takes it back to the folder / goes to the back instead of falling".
2. **Journey, his words.** "for each section in the site i want the card to come up and as you scroll it should go down to the next section but instead of cards i want them to be like folders where when you scroll to a section the cards for that section come out of the folder and show themselves as you scroll." The hero and the contact section are excluded.
3. **Energy curve.** Authored: the hero stays quiet (existing), the folders carry the motion, and the contact section is still.
4. **Feeling and the one moment.** See the curve and the peak below.
5. **The thing no other site does.** His idea: cards come out of a folder and get filed back in, shuffling horizontally "the same way the folders do".
6. **Range.** He chose **paper folders** in the site's carbon-copy palette, not glass on dark. Editorial and practical, not premium-minimal.
7. **One world or distinct scenes.** Distinct folders in one cabinet. Not a continuous world.
8. **Assets.** Nothing generated. Only the existing site images (Florece's Place, Cannon Thoughts).

He also decided:
- Earlier folders **tuck behind with their tab showing**, and the tabs work as navigation.
- Mobile uses the **same mechanic, rearranged**: the folder heading on top, the card at full width, and a sideways shuffle.
- Method gets **10 cards**, one per step.

## Feeling curve

```
1  Recognition   the hero's carbon-copy forms: their own paperwork, the pen catching a price (existing)
2  Trust (peak)  the first folder slides up and a real engagement comes out of it; each goes back in as the next comes out
3  Momentum      the method folder riffles: ten short cards on short steps
4  Confidence    builds come out with links you can open
5  Craft         websites, the only folder with pictures
6  Lightness     a thin folder of personal tools
7  Grounding     work history, short facts, then the cabinet lets go
8  Resolve       the contact plate stands alone, the email at display size (existing)
```

## Peak

"The section slid up like a folder, and the client case studies came out of it one at a time, then got filed back in." This is act 2, Clients. It has the largest per-card scroll on the page (80svh per card), and the hero before it is still.

## Tell-someone sentence

It's the site where every section is a folder landing on a pile, and the work comes out of it one sheet at a time and gets filed back.

## Authored silence

Each card holds still for the last 40% of its step, to give reading time. That is intended dwell, not dead scroll.
